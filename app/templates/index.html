Understanding YouTube CPM vs. RPM

How YouTube Monetization & Niche Revenue Work

CPM (Cost Per Mille) Advertiser Budget Metric

What advertisers pay YouTube for every 1,000 ad impressions. High-CPM niches (like Finance or AI) attract wealthy corporate advertisers willing to pay $25 - $50+ CPM.

RPM (Revenue Per Mille) Creator Net Earnings Metric

What YOU actually take home for every 1,000 total video views after YouTube takes its 45% cut and accounts for unmonetized views.

Real-World Revenue Comparison
  • 💰 High-CPM Niche (Finance / AI / Real Estate): $8,000 - $18,000 per 1M views
  • 🎮 Low-CPM Niche (Gaming / Memes / Vlogs): $1,000 - $2,000 per 1M views
